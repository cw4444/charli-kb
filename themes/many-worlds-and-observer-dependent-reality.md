# Many-Worlds, Wheeler, and Observer-Dependent Reality

This page ties Everett-style many-worlds into the existing observer-dependent-reality package. The short version is:

- Everett keeps the quantum formalism fully unitary and rejects collapse.
- This means Wigner, the friend, and the measured system can all be treated inside one universal wavefunction.
- The price is that there is no single absolute outcome at the global level.
- That does not mean "anything goes." It means outcomes are relative to branches or relative states.

If the existing package asks whether facts are observer-independent, Everett's answer is: not in the simple single-outcome sense. Facts are stable within branches, but there is no unique global outcome selected by collapse.

## The Basic Everett Move

Everett's 1957 relative-state formulation keeps unitary evolution everywhere. Measurement is not a special process that kills off all but one eigenstate. It is an interaction that correlates system, apparatus, and observer.

That matters here because the whole observer-dependent package is built around scenarios where one observer says "an outcome happened" while another can still treat the larger lab coherently. Everett says that is not a contradiction produced by bad bookkeeping. It is what universal quantum theory itself implies if collapse is not fundamental.

In that sense, Everett is not just adjacent to Wigner's friend. It is one of the cleanest ways to take Wigner's setup literally.

## Why Everett Fits Wigner's Friend So Naturally

Wigner's friend becomes awkward if you want two things at once:

- one universal quantum description, and
- one single globally absolute outcome.

Everett drops the second. The friend gets a definite result in a branch. Wigner can still describe the larger lab unitarily before interacting with it. There is no need to insert a Heisenberg cut where quantum evolution suddenly stops and classical outcome language takes over.

That is why many-worlds is a natural response to both [Wigner's Friend](wigners-friend.md) and [Frauchiger-Renner](frauchiger-renner.md). It preserves universal quantum theory by refusing the demand for one unique outcome across all perspectives.

## Wheeler's Participatory Universe Versus Everett

Wheeler and Everett are often mentioned in the same breath because both reject a simple pre-measurement classical picture. But they are not doing the same job.

Everett says:

- the universal wavefunction is real enough for physics,
- evolution is unitary,
- measurement does not select one unique world,
- observers are physical systems that become correlated with outcomes.

Wheeler's participatory-universe line pushes a different emphasis:

- observer-participancy is not just a practical feature of science,
- information and registered yes/no answers matter deeply,
- the articulation of reality may depend on acts of observation or registration,
- the slogan is more philosophical and programmatic than theorem-like.

So Everett is the cleaner formal move, while Wheeler is the stronger metaphysical mood. If you are trying to understand Bell, Brukner, local friendliness, or Frauchiger-Renner, Everett gives a more explicit technical response. Wheeler gives a bigger frame about why passive-observer realism may have been the wrong starting point.

## Everett Versus QBism

[QBism, Global Constraints, and Observer-Dependent Reality](qbism-adlam-observer-dependent-reality.md) already covers the QBist side. The sharp contrast with Everett is:

- Everett treats the quantum state as part of the physical story.
- QBism treats the quantum state as an agent's expectation structure.
- Everett keeps an objective universal formalism and relaxes single outcomes.
- QBism keeps single personal experiences but denies that the quantum state is objective furniture of the world.

Both reject the naive idea of one observer-independent ledger sitting underneath every measurement. But they reject it in very different ways.

If you are an Everett partisan, the QBist move can feel too psychologized. If you are a QBist, Everett can look like an ontology explosion used to save an over-literal reading of the wavefunction.

## Everett Versus Relational Quantum Mechanics

[Relational Quantum Mechanics](interpretations.md#relational-quantum-mechanics) is closer to Everett than QBism is, but still distinct.

- Everett says the full wavefunction evolves unitarily and multiple outcomes persist in branch-relative structure.
- Rovelli says properties are only definite relative to other systems, not absolutely.
- Everett multiplies outcome structure.
- RQM relativizes value assignments inside one quantum world.

Both resist absolute observer-independent property ascription. But RQM does not need the many-worlds branching picture, and Everett does not define facts primarily as relations between systems.

One rough way to say it: RQM is relation-first, Everett is wavefunction-first.

## Everett Versus Consistent Histories

Consistent histories is another close neighbor that belongs in this lane.

- It also removes measurement from the role of fundamental miracle.
- It treats quantum reasoning as framework-dependent.
- It forbids combining incompatible frameworks into one all-at-once story.
- It does not require a single universally true state of affairs at every instant.

That makes it "same font" as observer-dependent reality, but with a different style of discipline. Everett says all branches persist in the universal state. Consistent histories says you must reason within one consistent framework at a time and not mix incompatible ones like a quantum salad bar.

If Everett is generous about ontology, consistent histories is strict about allowed description.

## Frauchiger-Renner And Local Friendliness

Frauchiger-Renner pressures any interpretation that wants:

- universal validity of quantum theory,
- cross-agent consistency of reasoning, and
- single outcomes.

Everett's obvious move is to reject single global outcome. So the theorem is not a death blow to Everett; it is pressure against single-world uses of universal unitary quantum theory.

Local friendliness similarly targets the absoluteness of observed events. Everett again has a ready answer: observed events are absolute only within a branch, not across the full universal state.

This does not make Everett cost-free. It simply means Everett pays the cost in multiplicity rather than in collapse, hidden variables, or agent-centered probabilities.

## Wheeler, Information-Theoretic Approaches, and Pragmatist Copenhagen-ish Views

There is another cluster nearby that should not be collapsed into Everett:

- Wheeler's participatory universe and "it from bit"
- Zeilinger/Brukner-style information-theoretic emphasis
- pragmatist and Copenhagen-ish views that treat the formalism as a guide to what can be said, predicted, or registered

These views share a suspicion of naive microscopic picture-thinking. But they are usually less committed than Everett to a single objective universal wavefunction carrying all branches.

Everett says: take the formalism seriously as world-structure.

Wheeler/information-first/pragmatist views more often say: take information, registration, intervention, or disciplined description seriously before you turn the formalism into a hidden picture of what reality "really is underneath."

That is why Wheeler belongs in this package, but not as a disguised many-worlds theorist.

## Von Neumann Chain And Heisenberg Cut

The von Neumann chain asks where the measurement interaction ends: system, apparatus, friend, Wigner, larger environment, and so on.

The Heisenberg cut is the move of placing a boundary between quantum system and classical observer/apparatus.

Everett's appeal is that it removes the need for a fundamental cut. The chain does not need a magical stop point. It is quantum all the way up.

Wheeler keeps pressure on the idea that observation/registration matters. Pragmatist and Copenhagen-ish views often keep some version of the cut as an operational boundary. Consistent histories tries to dissolve the paradox by changing the rules of quantum reasoning. QBism personalizes outcomes. RQM relativizes them. Everett universalizes the quantum treatment and accepts branching.

## Markus Muller And Observer-First Reconstructions

If the question is "what else is in the same font as observer-dependent reality?", Markus Muller's observer-first work belongs on the outer edge of the cluster.

It goes further than Wheeler by treating observer-state or first-person structure as primitive and letting something like objective world-structure emerge from constraints on observation. That is not standard quantum interpretation, but it clearly rhymes with:

- participatory-universe thinking,
- anti-view-from-nowhere thinking,
- information-first reconstructions,
- and attempts to derive public reality from constraints on observation rather than assuming it at the start.

It is a useful fringe neighbor, not the center of the current Bell/Wigner package.

## Working Summary

If you like Everett, the package reads cleanly once you say the following out loud:

- Bell pressures local hidden-variable realism.
- Wigner's friend pressures single-outcome talk under universal quantum theory.
- Frauchiger-Renner pressures single-world consistency when agents reason about each other.
- Local friendliness pressures absoluteness of observed events.
- Everett answers by keeping unitary quantum theory and denying one unique global outcome.
- Wheeler belongs nearby because he radicalizes the role of observer-participancy and information, but he is less technically precise than Everett.

That is the tie-in. Everett is not outside the observer-dependent-reality package. It is one of its most natural internal endpoints.
