# Frauchiger-Renner

Frauchiger and Renner's 2018 paper, "Quantum Theory Cannot Consistently Describe the Use of Itself," is an extended Wigner's-friend thought experiment about agents using quantum theory to reason about other agents who are also using quantum theory.

## The Three Assumptions

The theorem is built around three assumptions:

- Q: quantum theory can be applied universally and its probability-1 predictions can be trusted.
- C: agents' reasoning is mutually consistent.
- S: each measurement has a single outcome.

The paper argues that these cannot all hold in the proposed protocol.

## Relation To Brukner

Frauchiger-Renner and Brukner both pressure global, observer-independent fact assignment. But they do so differently:

- Frauchiger-Renner uses nested agents and logical certainty claims.
- Brukner uses a Bell-type inequality in an extended Wigner's-friend setup.
- Local friendliness further reformulates the issue around absoluteness of observed events and local agency.

## Critiques

Common objections include:

- The agents may be combining incompatible perspectives.
- The reasoning may illicitly mix collapsed and uncollapsed descriptions.
- The isolation of measurement-like processes may be physically suspect.
- Bohmian, many-worlds, relational, QBist, and collapse interpretations each reject a different assumption or inference.

Everett's move is especially direct: preserve universal validity of the quantum formalism and drop the demand for one unique global outcome. See [Many-Worlds, Wheeler, and Observer-Dependent Reality](many-worlds-and-observer-dependent-reality.md).

Lazarovici and Hubert argue that a complete description removes the inconsistency, especially for Bohmian mechanics. Steane argues that reasoning from observations must account for quantum erasure and that the thought experiment does not simply separate single-world from many-world views.

## Why It Matters

Frauchiger-Renner is less directly experimental than Proietti or local friendliness, but it is central to the philosophical issue: can quantum theory be used by agents inside the world to reason about other agents without producing contradictions?
