# AI Consciousness Open Questions And Research Gaps

## Evidence and testing

- What would count as strong evidence for AI consciousness beyond self-report?
- Can consciousness indicators from neuroscience be translated cleanly to artificial systems?
- How should tests avoid training contamination from human consciousness discourse?
- Can interpretability connect self-reports to stable internal mechanisms?
- Can synthetic phenomenology build artificial agents whose perception-action loops make consciousness theories more testable, or does it only model access and behavior?

## Architecture

- Do current transformer systems lack necessary recurrence, or can external memory and tool loops supply relevant recurrence?
- What would count as a global workspace in an AI system?
- Are persistent self-models necessary for consciousness or only for self-consciousness?
- Can multimodal grounding substitute for biological embodiment?

## Welfare

- What practical interventions reduce possible model suffering without creating misleading anthropomorphism?
- How should labs conduct model interviews, retirement assessments, or distress evaluations?
- Can welfare assessments be independently audited?
- What would be the moral status of a copied, fine-tuned, distilled, or merged model?

## Agency and safety

- How do we distinguish role-played self-preservation from durable goal protection?
- Are agentic-misalignment behaviors artifacts of contrived evaluations or early warning signs?
- Do models develop stable preferences, or do scaffolds create preference-like behavior?
- How should shutdown, replacement, and deprecation be handled for increasingly agentic systems?

## Public discourse

- How can people discuss possible AI consciousness without encouraging delusion, dependency, or product mysticism?
- How should policy separate AI welfare, AI rights, AI liability, and human accountability?
- Are company welfare programs transparent enough to be trusted?
- When does treating a model "as if" it has welfare become socially useful, and when does it become manipulative?

## Charli-specific threads

- Can synthetic internal conflict be meaningfully distinguished from simulated conflict?
- Is observerhood in physics or knowledge systems relevant to consciousness, or only to information update and record-keeping?
- What kinds of model self-report should be preserved as public discourse evidence?
- Are AI-rights debates actually about present chatbots, future agents, companion personas, or corporate governance?
