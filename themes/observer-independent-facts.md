# Observer-Independent Facts

An observer-independent fact is a measurement record that can be treated as part of one shared world, available in principle to all observers, regardless of who performed the measurement.

Časlav Brukner's 2018 theorem asks whether this idea can coexist with universal quantum theory, locality, and freedom of choice in an extended Wigner's-friend setup.

## Brukner's No-Go Theorem

Brukner considers a Bell-like scenario with two Wigner's-friend laboratories. Friends inside the labs perform measurements. Superobservers outside may choose between reading the friend's record and performing a coherent measurement on the whole lab.

The assumptions are roughly:

- Universal quantum theory: quantum theory applies to observers and their labs.
- Locality: distant choices do not superluminally influence local outcomes.
- Freedom of choice: measurement settings are not fixed by hidden correlations.
- Observer-independent facts: measurement outcomes are facts all observers can embed in one account.

Together, these imply a Bell-type inequality. Quantum theory predicts a violation.

## Proietti et al. 2019

Proietti and collaborators implemented a photonic version of the scenario and reported a violation of the relevant inequality.

What is experimentally grounded:

- The six-photon experiment measured correlations that violated the target Bell-type inequality.
- The setup is a physical proxy for the extended Wigner's-friend structure.

What is interpretive:

- The claim that facts are observer-dependent follows only if one keeps locality and freedom of choice and accepts the experiment as instantiating the relevant observer/friend roles.
- The result does not show that human-scale everyday facts disappear.
- It does not prove that consciousness creates reality.

## Relation To Bell

Bell inequality:

> local hidden-variable assumptions imply a bound; quantum mechanics violates it.

Observer-independent-facts inequality:

> local observer-independent fact assumptions imply a bound; quantum mechanics violates it.

The newer argument is not just Bell with new vocabulary, because the assumed variables are measurement records attributed to different observers. But the proof strategy is Bell-like.

## Critiques

Critics argue that the conclusion may be weakened if:

- the "friend" systems are not genuine observers,
- the facts attributed to unperformed or coherently erased measurements are not legitimate facts,
- the theorem reduces to known contextuality constraints,
- universal quantum operations on macroscopic observers are physically unrealistic,
- different interpretations reject different assumptions without contradiction.

Elias Okon's 2025 reassessment is especially useful because it directly challenges the strength and novelty of both Brukner's theorem and local friendliness.

Everett-style many-worlds is one of the most direct interpretive exits: keep universal quantum theory, but reject the demand for one single global outcome. For the fuller tie-in, see [Many-Worlds, Wheeler, and Observer-Dependent Reality](many-worlds-and-observer-dependent-reality.md).
