# Rovelli, Relational Quantum Mechanics, and Reality

Carlo Rovelli probably deserves a proper page in this wiki.

Not because he is a celebrity physicist, but because relational quantum mechanics is one of the clearest attempts in the current package to say what kind of thing a fact is if physics does not grant us a single absolute ledger of properties.

## The Core RQM Move

Relational quantum mechanics says physical properties are not simply there in themselves, waiting to be listed from nowhere. A value, event, or state attribution is always relative to some physical system.

That does not mean "anything goes." It means the world is not described by one final inventory of intrinsic properties possessed absolutely by each thing. What exists physically is articulated through interactions and the relations they establish.

If Everett is wavefunction-first, RQM is relation-first.

## Why Rovelli Is More Useful Here Than Feynman

Feynman is useful for a narrow but important reason: he marks the boundary between calculation and explanatory story.

Rovelli is useful for a deeper reason in this repo: he is actually trying to answer the question you care about. If facts are not absolute, what are they? If knowledge is always from somewhere, what makes reality stable enough not to collapse into mush?

That makes Rovelli a better anchor for the reality obsession itself.

## RQM And The Observer-Dependent Reality Package

RQM fits the current package cleanly because it threads between several positions already in the repo.

- It is closer to QBism than Bohm or objective collapse in rejecting an absolute state description.
- It is less agent-centered than QBism because the relevant relata are physical systems, not primarily betting agents.
- It is less ontologically expansive than Everett because it does not lean on one universal wavefunction plus branching worlds.
- It is less slogan-heavy than Wheeler because it offers a more explicit interpretation rather than a participatory metaphysical mood.

This makes it one of the cleanest homes for "facts can be relational without becoming magical."

## Facts Without A God's-Eye Ledger

The reason RQM matters is that it redefines what a fact is allowed to be.

In a classical picture, a fact is something like:

- a property a thing has,
- whether or not anybody checks,
- in the same way for all possible describers.

RQM loosens that picture. A fact is something more like:

- an event or value established in an interaction,
- relative to the systems involved,
- with wider stability emerging only when relations cohere across broader physical contexts.

This is exactly why RQM belongs in a wiki about observer-dependent facts rather than just in a generic interpretations list.

## Why Your Yoneda Hunch Is Useful

Your Yoneda analogy is not crazy. It is actually a good thinking tool if it stays at the level of "same font" rather than identity.

The useful resonance is:

- you do not grasp a thing through a naked essence isolated from all relations,
- you grasp it through the structured pattern of how it shows up in relation to other things,
- and what can be said about it is bound to those relational interfaces.

That is not a claim that RQM is category theory in disguise. It is a conceptual rhyme:

- no intrinsic view from nowhere,
- structure revealed through relations,
- identity or describability tied to the network of possible interactions rather than a sealed inner core.

That is a strong bridge for thought, especially for your broader reality thread.

## Where The Analogy Must Stop

This is where discipline matters.

- Yoneda is a mathematical result in category theory.
- RQM is a physical interpretation of quantum mechanics.
- The analogy is useful because both push against naive intrinsic essence-talk.
- The analogy becomes sloppy if it pretends Rovelli is secretly doing category theory or that category theory proves RQM.

So the safe version is: same font, not same theorem.

## RQM And Neuroscience

This page also connects naturally to the repo's neuroscience lane.

The connection is not that quantum mechanics explains perception. The connection is epistemic.

In both RQM and the reality-threshold material, what matters is the refusal of a naive picture where the world simply arrives pre-labeled and the mind or system passively reads it off.

- In RQM, physical facts are interaction-relative.
- In reality-threshold work, felt realness is a judgment built from signal strength, context, and source discrimination.

These are different domains, but they point in the same direction: relation, interaction, and structured access matter more than naive intrinsic givenness.

## RQM Versus Nearby Views

RQM is easiest to place by contrast.

### RQM versus QBism

- QBism: quantum states are an agent's expectations.
- RQM: states and values are relative to physical systems.
- QBism personalizes.
- RQM relationalizes more generally.

### RQM versus Everett

- Everett: universal wavefunction, no collapse, branch-relative outcomes.
- RQM: no absolute state description in the first place.
- Everett keeps the global structure and relativizes outcomes within it.
- RQM is more suspicious of the global structure itself.

### RQM versus Wheeler

- Wheeler emphasizes observer-participancy and information as a philosophical mood.
- Rovelli gives a sharper account of how facts might be relational without requiring a cosmic participatory slogan.

## Do Not Overclaim

- Do not say RQM means all truth is local opinion.
- Do not say RQM abolishes reality. It revises what kind of reality quantum physics licenses.
- Do not say relation-dependence means consciousness creates facts.
- Do not say Rovelli has solved every consistency problem about public reality.

## Charli's Working Interpretation

This is interpretation/speculation, not an established result:

RQM may be one of the best fits in the repo for the intuition that reality is not made of little sealed nuggets carrying full identity in themselves. A thing becomes describable through the structured way it enters into relations, records, constraints, and interactions.

That is why the Yoneda comparison feels alive. Not because RQM literally is category theory, but because both resist the fantasy that you can know a thing in pure isolation from its web of relations.

If Feynman is the warning that calculation is not the same as metaphysics, Rovelli is the proposal that metaphysics itself may need to become relational.
