# Interpretations

Bell, Wigner's friend, Brukner, and local friendliness do not force one interpretation. They force a cost. Different interpretations choose different costs.

## QBism

QBism treats quantum states as an agent's personal probability assignments, not objective physical states. A measurement outcome is an experience for the agent.

- Fits observer-dependent facts naturally.
- Rejects the need for one observer-independent quantum state.
- Risk: can sound too private unless intersubjective agreement is explained carefully.

Source: Fuchs and Schack 2013, https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.85.1693

See also: [QBism, Global Constraints, and Observer-Dependent Reality](qbism-adlam-observer-dependent-reality.md).

## All-At-Once / Global-Constraint Views

Emily Adlam's related work on laws as constraints, temporal nonlocality, and global determinism is not an observer-dependent interpretation in the same sense as QBism. Its useful role in this package is different: it questions whether physical explanation must be local forward evolution through time.

- Fits block-universe-adjacent and retrocausal/global-constraint questions.
- Can challenge simple locality and time-evolution assumptions behind some no-go-theorem intuitions.
- Should not be treated as saying observers create reality.

Sources: Adlam 2018, 2021, 2022 entries in [Source Index](../sources/source-index.md#adlam-2022---laws-of-nature-as-constraints).

## Relational Quantum Mechanics

Relational quantum mechanics says states and properties are relative to physical systems.

- Fits the idea that facts can be relational.
- Does not require consciousness.
- Key challenge: explaining stable shared reality across observers.

Source: Rovelli 1996, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02302261

## Copenhagen / Collapse-Style Views

Copenhagen-style views often place a boundary between quantum system and classical measurement context. Collapse-style views treat outcome selection as real or at least operationally fundamental.

- They preserve single observed outcomes.
- They may deny that Wigner can treat the whole lab as just another quantum system.
- Cost: the quantum/classical boundary or collapse rule needs explanation.

## Many-Worlds / Everett

Many-worlds keeps universal unitary quantum mechanics and rejects single global outcomes.

- Avoids Wigner-friend contradictions by allowing all outcomes in branches.
- "Facts" are branch-relative.
- Cost: probability, preferred-basis, and ontology questions.

Source: Everett 1957, https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.29.454

## Objective Collapse

Objective collapse modifies quantum dynamics so macroscopic superpositions physically collapse.

- Preserves observer-independent outcomes.
- Avoids universal Wigner measurements on large labs.
- Cost: modifies standard quantum theory and faces experimental constraints.

Source: GRW 1986, https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.34.470

## Bohmian Mechanics

Bohmian mechanics preserves definite particle configurations guided by the wavefunction.

- Single-world and realist.
- Explicitly nonlocal, so it does not satisfy Bell locality.
- Critics of Frauchiger-Renner often use Bohmian mechanics to show that single-world theories need not be inconsistent.

Source: Lazarovici and Hubert 2019, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-37535-1

## Superdeterminism

Superdeterminism rejects measurement-setting independence: choices of settings are not statistically independent of the hidden variables.

- Can evade Bell-style and local-friendliness constraints by rejecting free choice/local agency assumptions.
- Cost: highly controversial and difficult to make scientifically productive.

Source: Hossenfelder and Palmer 2020, https://doi.org/10.3389/fphy.2020.00139

## Wheeler / Participatory Universe

Wheeler's participatory universe is philosophically adjacent: observers and information are central to how physical reality is articulated.

- Useful for framing questions about observer participation.
- Not a substitute for Bell, Brukner, or local-friendliness theorems.
- Treat as inspiration, not evidence.

## Practical Summary

If you want observer-dependent reality, QBism and relational quantum mechanics are the cleanest conceptual neighbors. If you want one absolute world, objective collapse or Bohmian mechanics are more natural, but they pay different prices. If you want universal unitary evolution, many-worlds is natural, but facts become branch-relative.
