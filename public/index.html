Bell, Wigner's Friend, and Observer-Dependent Reality

A readable map of how Bell inequalities connect to Brukner's no-go theorem, Proietti's local observer-independence experiment, local friendliness, and interpretations where facts may be relational rather than absolute.

Core Takeaway

Bell-style arguments turn assumptions into inequalities. Bell targets local hidden-variable explanations. Brukner and local-friendliness work adapt the strategy to facts: can all observed outcomes be placed into one observer-independent ledger while preserving locality, free choice, and universal quantum mechanics?

Do not overread Proietti et al. 2019. The experiment reports a Bell-type inequality violation in a photonic proxy setup. The stronger conclusion that facts are observer-dependent requires additional assumptions, especially locality and free choice, and does not directly show that everyday macroscopic facts disappear.
