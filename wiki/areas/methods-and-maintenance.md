## The Inclusion Test

Keep a source when it supplies a missing foundation, a durable event, a useful mechanism, or a bridge that genuinely clarifies two existing lanes. If it is merely interesting for twenty loud minutes, it can stay in conversation or `raw/` until it proves it deserves shelf space.
