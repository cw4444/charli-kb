# Optimism And Behaviour

Start here for future expectation, emotion regulation, habits, attention, and practical agency. The house rule is simple: useful optimism stays in contact with evidence; compulsory cheerfulness can get in the sea.

## Start With

- [Optimism](../concepts/optimism.md) for the main map: future simulation, expectation, belief updating, attention, agency, and the limits of positive bias.
- [Research - Optimism](../questions/research-optimism.md) for the fuller evidence and open questions.
- [Habits, Goals, And Behavioural Control](../concepts/habits-goals-and-behavioral-control.md) for turning a desired future into actual behaviour rather than a decorative thought.

## When You Have A New Paper

Ask whether it concerns a stable trait, a task-specific bias, a short intervention, a health association, or a mechanism. Those are neighbours, not interchangeable facts wearing similar hats.
