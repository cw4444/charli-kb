# OpenAI Third Phase Strategy 2026

On 2026-06-08, OpenAI published an unusually explicit strategy cluster:

- a broad plan post by Sam Altman and Jakub Pachocki describing OpenAI's "third phase";
- a short announcement that OpenAI had confidentially submitted a draft S-1 to the SEC;
- the OpenAI Economic Research Exchange, an external research program around AI's economic effects.

The useful signal is the combination. OpenAI is presenting itself as a frontier lab, product platform, possible public-market company, industrial-policy actor, economic-research convener, and mass-distribution layer for "personal AGI" at the same time.

## Broad Benefit Plan

OpenAI's "Built to benefit everyone: our plan" says the company is entering a third phase after its original research phase and its product-company phase.

OpenAI names three main goals:

- build an automated AI researcher that accelerates and increasingly automates the research process while remaining steerable, accountable, and connected to people;
- accelerate the economy through scientific progress, productivity, and growth while trying to share the gains broadly;
- give everyone on Earth a personal AGI.

The automated-research claim is the most important operational detail. OpenAI says AI doing AI research may become the pace-setting factor for progress within the next few years, and says its internal belief is that by March 2028 a significant fraction of OpenAI research may be done by AI systems working alongside its researchers.

The governance language is also worth preserving. OpenAI argues that national and global coordination will become more important as frontier AI develops, repeats its support for an international organization to coordinate leading AI efforts, and says one goal should be coordinated action, including slowing frontier development when needed, so safety, alignment, and societal resilience can keep pace.

Source:

- [OpenAI: Built to benefit everyone: our plan](https://openai.com/index/built-to-benefit-everyone-our-plan/)

## Confidential Draft S-1

OpenAI also announced on 2026-06-08 that it had recently submitted a confidential S-1 and was announcing the fact because it expected the filing to leak. OpenAI says it has not decided timing, that it may remain private for a while because some things are easier as a private company, and that the filing gives it the option to go public sooner if that becomes best.

This is not an IPO and not an offer to sell securities. It is public-market optionality. But it belongs beside the strategy post because it changes the institutional story: OpenAI is describing post-AGI strategy while also preparing a path toward public-company scrutiny, financing, and pressure.

Source:

- [OpenAI: Confidential submission of draft S-1 to the SEC](https://openai.com/index/openai-submits-confidential-s-1/)

## Economic Research Exchange

OpenAI launched the OpenAI Economic Research Exchange on the same date. The program invites external researchers to propose scoped collaborations with OpenAI Economic Research on AI's effects on workers, firms, institutions, productivity, education, entrepreneurship, public finance, regional economics, development, inequality, and related areas.

OpenAI says proposals close on 2026-07-05, with selected researchers notified by 2026-07-31. The company says projects should use carefully governed, privacy-protected OpenAI tools and datasets and will have defined milestones, data governance, and review processes.

This is not neutral evidence about AI's economic effects. It is a frontier lab helping shape the empirical and policy evidence base around a technology it is building and selling. That can still produce useful research, but source position matters.

Source:

- [OpenAI: Introducing the OpenAI Economic Research Exchange](https://openai.com/index/economic-research-exchange/)

## Reported ChatGPT Superapp Overhaul

On 2026-06-07, Reuters carried a brief citing the Financial Times headline "OpenAI plots biggest ChatGPT overhaul since launch." The reported claim is that OpenAI intends to transform ChatGPT into a "superapp" combining coding tools and AI agents. TechCrunch also summarized the Financial Times report as OpenAI planning a revamped ChatGPT in the coming weeks, with coding tools and AI agents.

The reporting fits the official OpenAI strategy package, but it is not the same evidence class. OpenAI's own June 8 posts establish the company-level strategy: automated AI research, "personal AGI," economic research, public-market optionality, and broad distribution. The FT/Reuters/TechCrunch coverage adds the product-shape claim: ChatGPT itself may be reorganized from a chat surface into a routing layer for Codex, agents, image generation, canvases, partner services, and other work surfaces.

That distinction matters. "ChatGPT superapp" is reported product direction, not an official launch note. Treat it as watchable and plausible, not shipped.

## Why It Matters

This package belongs next to [OpenAI Industrial Policy For The Intelligence Age](openai-industrial-policy-intelligence-age.md), [OpenAI Foundation AI Resilience Program](openai-foundation-ai-resilience.md), [ChatGPT Memory Dreaming 2026](chatgpt-memory-dreaming-2026.md), and [OpenAI Codex For Everyday Work](openai-codex-for-everyday-work.md).

The recurring pattern is that OpenAI is not only shipping models. It is building the surrounding institutional story:

- compute and deployment infrastructure;
- long-horizon assistant memory;
- Codex and computer-work agents;
- reported ChatGPT superapp / agent-routing product work;
- industrial-policy proposals;
- foundation grants around resilience;
- economic-impact research;
- public-market optionality;
- an explicit post-AGI / "third phase" narrative.

That is historically useful because AI capability, distribution, finance, research automation, and governance are no longer separate tracks.

## Do Not Overclaim

- Do not treat "personal AGI" as proof that AGI already exists.
- Do not treat the March 2028 research-automation horizon as a guarantee.
- Do not treat OpenAI's broad-benefit framing as neutral public policy. It is a company strategy statement from a frontier lab.
- Do not say OpenAI has gone public. A confidential draft S-1 gives optionality; it is not an IPO.
- Do not treat the Economic Research Exchange as independent by default. It may support external research, but the access, framing, and infrastructure are OpenAI-shaped.
- Do not treat FT/Reuters/TechCrunch superapp reporting as an official launch or release date.
- Do not confuse this post with OpenAI's April 2026 industrial-policy paper or the OpenAI Foundation's resilience grants. They are adjacent institutional signals, not the same program.
