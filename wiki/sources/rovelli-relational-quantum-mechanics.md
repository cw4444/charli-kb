# Rovelli And Relational Quantum Mechanics

## Metadata

- Topic: Carlo Rovelli's relational quantum mechanics as a serious interpretation for observer-dependent or relation-dependent facts.
- Access note: this page uses public primary and scholarly reference sources and summarizes them in original words.

## Primary-Source Claims

Rovelli's 1996 paper proposes that quantum states and values of physical quantities are not absolute features a system simply has by itself. They are relative to other physical systems. The interpretation rejects a God's-eye inventory of intrinsic properties and instead treats quantum mechanics as describing what can be said about systems in relation to one another.

Rovelli's later 2021 overview sharpens the point by describing the ontology in terms of sparse relative events or "facts" realized in interactions. On this view, facts are not universal entries in one master ledger. They are facts for some interacting system relative to another, and the approximation of stable public reality emerges where decoherence hides interference well enough.

The 2019 locality paper with Martin-Dussaud and Zalamea is useful because it shows RQM is not just a poetic claim that "everything is connected." It is an attempt to preserve locality in a relational key by rethinking what counts as a physical description, rather than by adding hidden global furniture.

## Useful Synthesis

The durable RQM move is not merely "observers matter." It is more specific:

- there is no view from nowhere inside the formalism,
- physical properties are not absolute monadic possessions,
- events and state ascriptions are relational,
- and stable shared reality has to be explained as an achieved coherence across relations, not assumed at the start.

That makes RQM more useful for this wiki than generic observer-mysticism. It gives a precise way to resist simple observer-independent fact talk without jumping straight to consciousness-causes-collapse or to a many-worlds branching ontology.

## Relevance To Charli's Research

RQM is a strong fit for the reality lane because it speaks directly to the kind of question Charli keeps asking:

- What is a fact if there is no God's-eye ledger?
- How can reality be structured, constrained, and public enough for science without being absolutely perspective-free?
- What does it mean to know a thing if what is knowable is always mediated by interaction, relation, and record?

This makes Rovelli more central to the repo's actual obsession than Feynman. Feynman is useful as a brake on interpretive overreach. Rovelli is useful as a positive proposal about what kind of thing a physical fact is.

## Tensions And Open Problems

- Inter-observer consistency is the main pressure point. If facts are relative, what exactly explains the stability of the shared world?
- RQM can sound close to QBism from a distance, but QBism is agent-centered and probability-centered while RQM is system-to-system relational and not centered on personal belief.
- RQM can sound close to Everett from a distance, but Everett keeps a universal wavefunction while RQM resists the idea of a global absolute state description.
- Critics argue that RQM does not fully dissolve the measurement problem, only relocates it into consistency conditions across perspectives.

## Do Not Overclaim

- Do not flatten RQM into "reality is subjective."
- Do not say Rovelli proves that facts are invented by observers.
- Do not treat "observer" in RQM as meaning only conscious humans; the point is broader physical interaction.
- Do not say RQM and category theory are literally the same framework.
