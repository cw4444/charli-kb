# EU Technological Sovereignty Package 2026

On 2026-06-03, the European Commission presented a European Technological Sovereignty Package spanning semiconductors, artificial intelligence, cloud, open source, and energy-system digitalisation.

The package includes:

- a Chips Act 2.0 legislative proposal;
- a Cloud and AI Development Act legislative proposal;
- an Open Source Strategy;
- a Strategic Roadmap for Digitalisation and AI in Energy.

The Commission frames these measures as a response to Europe's dependence on non-EU suppliers for core digital technologies and to rising demand for computing capacity as AI spreads.

Source:

- [European Commission: Commission proposes tech sovereignty package to strengthen Europe's digital autonomy and resilience](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-proposes-tech-sovereignty-package-strengthen-europes-digital-autonomy-and-resilience)

## Why It Matters

This is a durable industrial-policy and geopolitical signal. The EU is not treating AI as a standalone software product or a narrow regulation problem. It is linking AI capacity to chips, cloud, open-source infrastructure, energy, supply dependence, security, and public autonomy.

That belongs beside the wiki's existing U.S., G7, and EU governance anchors:

- the [White House Advanced AI Innovation And Security Order](white-house-ai-innovation-security-order.md) treats advanced-model evaluation and cyber capability as federal machinery;
- the G7 safe-AI-growth signal links AI security, trusted data flows, adoption, and resilient infrastructure;
- the EU AI Act Scientific Panel and Advisory Forum provide enforcement scaffolding;
- this package addresses the underlying capacity and dependency question.

## Do Not Overclaim

- Do not treat proposed legislation as enacted law.
- Do not treat technological sovereignty as technological self-sufficiency.
- Do not assume linking AI, cloud, chips, open source, and energy guarantees effective delivery.
- Do not flatten the EU, U.S., and G7 approaches into one governance model.
- Do not treat Commission framing as neutral analysis; this is an official policy proposal with strategic aims.
