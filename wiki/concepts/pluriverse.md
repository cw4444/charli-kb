# Pluriverse

The pluriverse is a reality-frame where the world is not treated as one completed view-from-nowhere, but as something articulated through many situated perspectives that interact, update, constrain one another, and sometimes agree.

In this wiki, use "pluriverse" narrowly. It is a bridge concept, not a new grand ontology. Its value is that it gives Charli's reality lane a name for a recurring pattern:

- reality is not just private imagination;
- reality is not necessarily a single God's-eye inventory;
- observers, agents, measurements, records, bodies, and models matter;
- personal perspective is real enough to do work, but not sovereign enough to ignore constraint.

The newest wrinkle is memory. A local screenshot of an Eric Cavalcanti X post, preserved at `raw/cavalcanti.png`, puts the problem plainly: some experiences are not remembered, and some remembered experiences did not happen. That sits neatly between the [Reality Threshold](reality-threshold.md) question and the Wigner/local-friendliness question. The felt event, the remembered event, and the public record are not automatically the same thing.

Charli's *Black Mirror: Bete Noire* connection is useful as fiction, not evidence. Maria is the only one who remembers an original reality after Verity uses a pendant-linked "quantum compiler" to move through realities where Verity's claims have always been true. The episode borrows the emotional shape of many-worlds language: not changing one world, but selecting a parallel reality where the desired claim already holds. That is the nightmare version of this page's sober point: private continuity, memory, social consensus, and public record can be pulled apart. The episode is a metaphor, not a physics source. Obviously. Apparently that has to be said.

## Why It Is Not The Multiverse

The pluriverse is almost the opposite of the usual popular multiverse picture.

Many-worlds / Everett says the universal quantum state evolves without collapse, and what looks like one outcome is branch-relative. Its price is a very large branch structure.

The pluriverse frame is not primarily about parallel branches. It is about interlocking first-person or agent-relative perspectives inside one shared resistant world. The important question is not "how many worlds split off?" but "how do situated perspectives stitch a public world together?"

That makes the pluriverse a neighbor of QBism, participatory realism, relational quantum mechanics, phenomenology, and predictive processing. It is not a synonym for any one of them.

## Relation To QBism

QBism is the strongest physics anchor for this page.

In QBism, a quantum state is not an objective physical state sitting in the world. It is an agent's disciplined probability assignment about possible future experiences after acting on the world. The Born rule is a normative constraint on those assignments, not a hidden machine forcing nature's hand.

That matters because it makes quantum theory first-personal without making it arbitrary. The agent is in the world, acts on the world, is surprised by the world, and updates after experience.

This is why "personal reality" is both tempting and dangerous. The useful version is:

- each agent has a perspective;
- quantum-state assignment belongs to that perspective;
- experience updates the agent's model;
- communication and records can bring agents into agreement.

The daft version is:

- my mind invents electrons;
- anything I believe becomes true;
- physics is optional if the vibes are strong enough.

No. Put that down.

## Relation To Block Universe Thinking

The block universe is useful when it reminds us that relativity does not give every observer the same global "now." Events can be ordered differently across frames when they are spacelike separated, and there is no simple cosmic present sweeping across all reality.

But a fully static block picture can feel too finished: everything is already laid out, and agents merely trace their assigned world-lines.

The pluriverse frame pushes the other way. It keeps the situated present, updating, action, and first-person experience visible. It does not prove libertarian free will, but it resists treating lived reality as a dead spreadsheet.

For the global-constraint side, keep using [QBism, Global Constraints, and Observer-Dependent Reality](../../themes/qbism-adlam-observer-dependent-reality.md) and [Retrocausality, Delayed Choice, and Two Roads](../../themes/retrocausality-delayed-choice-and-two-roads.md). Adlam-style all-at-once lawhood is not the same move as QBism or pluriverse language.

## Relation To Predictive Processing

Predictive processing belongs here as the brain-level cousin.

Predictive-processing theories say perception is model-mediated. The brain uses expectations or generative models to predict sensory input, then updates when prediction errors arrive. Experience is not a raw camera feed from reality; it is an active, controlled, constantly revised model.

That sits neatly beside [Reality Threshold](reality-threshold.md), [Perception And Imagination Overlap](perception-and-imagination-overlap.md), and [Interface Theory Of Perception](interface-theory-of-perception.md):

- reality threshold: why an experience is judged real rather than imagined;
- predictive processing: how perception is guided by model and error correction;
- interface theory: why the experienced world may be useful without being literal;
- pluriverse: how many situated model-builders can still share a world.

Do not turn this into "neuroscience proves QBism." It does not. It just shows that the mind already works by model-mediated contact, not passive transparency.

## Do Not Overclaim

- Do not treat "pluriverse" as settled physics.
- Do not say Fuchs has pivoted away from QBism unless a primary source says so.
- Do not collapse QBism, RQM, many-worlds, Adlam, Hoffman, and predictive processing into one theory.
- Do not say personal perspective means private fantasy.
- Do not say intersubjective agreement is automatic. Shared reality has to be built through communication, records, calibration, and resistance from the world.
- Do not use this page as a shortcut to idealism, simulation theory, manifestation, or consciousness-causes-collapse.
- Do not use memory fallibility to pretend public evidence no longer matters. The whole point is to distinguish experience, memory, record, and world-resistance, not blend them into soup.

The sober version is plenty weird already. We needn't add glitter and call it science.
