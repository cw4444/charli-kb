# Hans Moravec

Hans Moravec is a roboticist associated in this wiki with early AI/posthumanist arguments about machine intelligence, robotics, mind uploading, and postbiological successors to humanity.

The immediate source is [Mind Children - Hans Moravec](../sources/mind-children-hans-moravec.md), a 1988 Harvard University Press book that frames future machine intelligence as potentially descending from or succeeding human intelligence.

## Relevance

- Historical imagination of AI before modern LLMs.
- Robotics-based route into artificial minds.
- Substrate independence and functional continuity.
- Mind uploading and continuity of self.
- Machine intelligences as possible descendants rather than mere tools.

## Caution

Use Moravec as a historical and conceptual source, not as proof that current systems are conscious or that mind uploading is technically solved.
