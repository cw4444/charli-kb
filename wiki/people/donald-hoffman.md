# Donald Hoffman

Donald Hoffman is a cognitive scientist associated here with the interface theory of perception: the idea that perception is more like a fitness-shaped user interface than a transparent copy of objective reality.

In `charli-kb`, Hoffman is useful because he gives a clean bridge between perception science and the repo's reality obsession. His desktop-icon analogy captures the point neatly: organisms may experience action-ready symbols rather than raw reality.

## Why He Matters Here

- He makes the "perception is an interface" claim memorable and research-linked.
- He connects evolutionary fitness, perception, and anti-naive-realism.
- He belongs beside [Reality Threshold](../concepts/reality-threshold.md) and [Perception And Imagination Overlap](../concepts/perception-and-imagination-overlap.md), because all three challenge the idea that felt reality is a simple readout of the world.
- He is adjacent to QBism/RQM/Adlam only as a perception-side neighbour, not as proof of quantum observer-dependence.

## Caveat

Hoffman's conscious-realism metaphysics is stronger and more controversial than the interface-theory point. Keep them separated. The wiki can use Hoffman as a perception/interface anchor without treating "consciousness is fundamental" as established fact.
