# Stefano Fusi

Stefano Fusi is useful to this wiki as a bridge figure for population-level representation, abstraction, and generalization.

He appears directly as a co-author on the 2026 *Nature Neuroscience* basolateral-amygdala paper summarized in [Representational Geometry In Brains And LLMs](../sources/representational-geometry-brains-and-llms.md). That paper matters here because it treats emotional-state variables as a population-geometry problem rather than a hunt for one cleanly labeled neuron.

In this wiki, Fusi's role is not "brain person vaguely adjacent to AI." The useful bridge is formal: meaningful variables can live in distributed relational structure, and generalization can depend on the geometry of population activity.

## Why He Matters Here

- He is part of the neuroscience side of the representational-geometry bridge.
- The work helps explain why mixed selectivity and clean variable readout are not contradictions.
- The abstraction/generalization frame gives the AI comparison something sturdier than metaphor.

## Do Not Overclaim

- Do not treat biological population geometry and SAE decoder geometry as the same measurement object.
- Do not turn population readout into subjective experience.
- Do not use a person page as a celebrity shelf. The point is the bridge, not the aura.
