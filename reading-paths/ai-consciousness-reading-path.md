# AI Consciousness Reading Path

## 1. Start with definitions

Read [Overview](../themes/ai-consciousness/overview.md) first. Keep consciousness, intelligence, agency, self-report, sentience, moral patienthood, and welfare separate.

Then read:

- David Chalmers, "Could a Large Language Model Be Conscious?"
- SEP, "Consciousness"
- SEP, "The Grounds of Moral Status"

## 2. Understand the serious pro-possibility case

Read:

- Butlin et al., "Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence"
- Long et al., "Taking AI Welfare Seriously"
- [Arguments for taking AI consciousness seriously](../themes/ai-consciousness/arguments-for.md)
- [Model welfare without assuming consciousness](../themes/ai-consciousness/model-welfare.md)

Focus on the difference between "possible / worth assessing" and "currently proven."

## 3. Read the skeptical case carefully

Read:

- Bender and Koller, "Climbing towards NLU"
- Bender et al., "On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots"
- Anil Seth, "Conscious Artificial Intelligence and Biological Naturalism"
- Birhane and van Dijk, "Robot Rights? Let's Talk about Human Welfare Instead"
- [Arguments against current AI consciousness](../themes/ai-consciousness/arguments-against.md)
- [Biological objections and embodiment arguments](../themes/ai-consciousness/biology-embodiment.md)

The skeptical case is not just "AI bad." It is a set of grounded objections about language, meaning, biology, embodiment, social harm, and evidence.

## 4. Add company and technical context

Read:

- Anthropic, "Exploring model welfare"
- Anthropic, "Claude's new constitution"
- Anthropic, "Mapping the Mind of a Large Language Model"
- Anthropic, "Persona vectors"
- Anthropic, "Sleeper Agents"
- Anthropic, "Alignment faking"
- Anthropic, "Agentic Misalignment"
- OpenAI Model Spec
- [Company positions](../themes/ai-consciousness/company-positions.md)
- [Interpretability and whether internal states matter](../themes/ai-consciousness/interpretability.md)
- [Agency, goals, self-models, and persistence](../themes/ai-consciousness/agency-self-models.md)

Read company sources as evidence and positioning at the same time.

## 5. Advanced synthesis

Read:

- Murray Shanahan, "Simulacra as Conscious Exotica"
- Leibo et al., "A Pragmatic View of AI Personhood"
- Schneider, "How to Catch an AI Zombie"
- [Substrate independence and functionalism](../themes/ai-consciousness/substrate-functionalism.md)
- [Moral patienthood and precautionary principles](../themes/ai-consciousness/moral-patienthood.md)
- [Open questions and research gaps](../themes/ai-consciousness/open-questions.md)

At this stage, the main question becomes governance under uncertainty: what should be assessed, what should be protected, and what language should public discourse avoid?
