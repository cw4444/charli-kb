# Beginner To Advanced Reading Path

## 1. Orientation

Start with the theme pages:

- [Bell Inequalities](../themes/bell-inequalities.md)
- [Wigner's Friend](../themes/wigners-friend.md)
- [Observer-Independent Facts](../themes/observer-independent-facts.md)

Goal: understand the difference between Bell nonlocality and observer-dependent facts.

## 2. Primary Foundations

Read:

- Bell 1964, "On the Einstein Podolsky Rosen Paradox"
- Wigner 1961, "Remarks on the Mind-Body Question"
- CHSH 1969, "Proposed Experiment to Test Local Hidden-Variable Theories"

Goal: see the original shapes of Bell and Wigner's friend before modern reinterpretation.

## 3. Modern No-Go Cluster

Read:

- Frauchiger and Renner 2018
- Brukner 2018
- Proietti et al. 2019
- Bong et al. 2020

Goal: understand how Wigner's friend becomes a Bell-style or consistency-style no-go theorem.

## 4. Interpretive Responses

Read selectively:

- Fuchs and Schack 2013 for QBism.
- Rovelli 1996 for relational quantum mechanics.
- Everett 1957 for relative states/many-worlds.
- GRW 1986 for objective collapse.
- Hossenfelder and Palmer 2020 for superdeterminism.

Goal: identify which assumption each interpretation rejects.

## 5. Critiques And Reassessments

Read:

- Lazarovici and Hubert 2019.
- Okon 2025.
- Steane 2025.
- Walleghem et al. 2025 if you want the contextuality connection.

Goal: avoid treating no-go theorems as settled metaphysical conclusions.

## 6. Charli Thread

Then read:

- [AI Observers](../themes/ai-observers.md)
- [Open Questions](../open-questions.md)
- [Concept Map](../maps/concept-map.md)

Goal: translate the physics into careful questions about relational facts, observers, AI systems, and public reality.
