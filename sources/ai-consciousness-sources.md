## Notes on source limits

- Some philosophy sources are paywalled or abstract-only. They are used for metadata and high-level claims only where no open full text was available.
- Company research is treated as important evidence but not neutral evidence.
- Journalism was used only as discovery and context, not as a core source for claims.
